About Arcade Edition

v2.0 combines the expanded grades 5–8 learning engine, 20 course environments, 64 illustrated motorcycle models, workshop and dealership, pit stops, trophy cabinet and optional earned sound. This is an illustrative game with estimated specifications, not real vehicle tuning guidance. All career data is stored locally; export a backup before switching devices.
